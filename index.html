Weekly reality check

FOX and CBS publish regional coverage maps about 12 days before each Sunday. 506 Sports maps them by market and will tell you exactly what Roanoke is getting before kickoff — the single best way to turn every "Maybe" above into a yes or no.

If the Michigan box happens

An HDHomeRun at your friend's place in the Detroit market plus Channels DVR flips nearly every Lions "Maybe" and "No" on this page to a yes — WJBK 2 carries the FOX games, WWJ 62 the CBS ones. A Green Bay or Milwaukee placement would do the same for the Packers.
